# Clinical Angle Convention

## Hip
Neutral approximately 0°. Flexion positive; extension negative.
The measure is a 2D trunk-referenced proxy.

## Knee
Neutral/straight knee approximately 0°. Flexion positive; hyperextension negative.

## Ankle
Neutral shank-foot relationship approximately 0°. Dorsiflexion positive;
plantarflexion negative.

## Camera-view requirement
These definitions assume a true or near-true sagittal view. Strong out-of-plane
rotation, frontal recording, landmark occlusion, and perspective distortion may
invalidate clinical interpretation.
